Before you begin

Set up your company

In this game, you are running a company selling computers. You have two competitors in this market, and your products are similar though not identical meaning customers are price sensitive but not fully elastic. You and your competitors simultaneously choose a price, and earn profits based on how the market assesses the three products and prices offered. You will play a one-shot game, and then a repeated game.
